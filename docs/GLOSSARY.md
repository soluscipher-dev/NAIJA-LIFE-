# Glossary

- **Faucet:** a source that creates money (jobs from NPC employers, rewards).
- **Sink:** something that removes money from the economy (rent, taxes, luxury, fees).
- **Ledger:** append-only record of every money movement (double-entry).
- **Idempotency key:** client-supplied unique key so a repeated request is processed once.
- **Escrow:** money held by the system until both sides finish a hustle gig.
- **Hustle:** player-to-player gig work.
- **Shift:** a timed work session at a career job.
- **Organization:** generic entity (company, ministry, police, court, party, media).
- **Presence:** who is online and where.
- **Kill switch:** feature flag to disable a risky feature instantly.
- **ADR:** Architecture Decision Record.
- **NDPA:** Nigeria Data Protection Act.
- **RBAC:** role-based access control.
- **PWA:** progressive web app (installable web app).
- **Gini coefficient:** measure of wealth inequality (tracked, not capped).
