# ADR-0006: No gambling, no cash-out

**Status:** Accepted  **Date:** 2026-10

## Context
Gambling brings licensing, app-store and legal risk. Cash-out creates fraud and regulatory risk.

## Decision
No real-money betting or loot boxes. No conversion of virtual currency to real money. Premium currency, if ever added, is cosmetic-only.

## Consequences
- Monetization is cosmetics, membership, seasons, and in-world ads.
