# ADR-0005: Uncapped wealth

**Status:** Accepted  **Date:** 2026-10

## Context
Wealth inequality is part of the fun. Hard caps feel artificial.

## Decision
No wealth cap. Control inflation with sinks the wealthy want (luxury, status, businesses) and a light admin-tunable tax. New-account transfer limits only.

## Consequences
- Ledger and anti-cheat integrity are critical.
- Economy dashboards are required before public launch.
